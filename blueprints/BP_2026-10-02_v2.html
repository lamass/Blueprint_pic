Leg

Hover a BP leg (click it to pin).

Controls

Move the whole BP: drag any BP line.
Adjust one joining point: drag the white dot. Dashed box = its allowance (amber = outside).
Price axis: drag up/down on the price labels to stretch/squeeze; double-click it for auto-fit.
Time axis: drag left/right on the time labels to stretch/squeeze; double-click it to fit all days.
Zoom: mouse wheel. Pan: drag the background.
Reset joints keeps the BP where you moved it; Reset BP (all) puts it back on its own day. The stored original is never changed.
